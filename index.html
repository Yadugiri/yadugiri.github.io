About

My day job is designing buildings: homes, schools, hospitals, shopping centres and mixed-use projects in India and the Gulf, from first sketch to construction drawings.

That work is full of slow, repetitive steps between an idea and something people can look at. Most of what's on this page started as an attempt to make one of those steps quicker.

Bachelor of Architecture, UVCE Bangalore. Outside work, I paint and make music.
