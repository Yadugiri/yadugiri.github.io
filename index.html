Prārūpa

Live

Photograph a hand-drawn floor plan and get a checked plan back. Once you approve it, you get a full set of concept and technical drawings, a 3D model you can walk around, pictures and a short video.

  • Walls, doors and windows drawn to building standards

  • Approve the plan before anything is built
